RINGGOOD, 2017, 16.2h bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

At the age of nine, Ringgood has now graduated to the status of warhorse, which in itself is something to admire. He is big, kind, friendly, elegant, a great mover, and with an adorable spotted blaze on his face. He is good with the farrier and is very special to his trainer who claims “he is one of the coolest horses we have ever had.” He said that Ringo, as they call him, has been a steady, friendly, and always hard-trying horse for him. He has become a family pet so they have thought about keeping him on as a pony but it is time for him to leave the track life. We were told that Ringo is still sound, has good feet and his only vice is he “dances in his stall.” He is easy to handle, calm and chill, and most interested in just “hanging out and grazing.” He is very respectful of his rider and is especially fond of women. While racing regularly, he could get stiff for a few days after a race, but warmed up out of it quickly. When we saw him and took his video, he showed no stiffness at all, and moved very freely from his shoulders with good hind end engagement — he’s a very good mover for sure!. Ringgood is by Teuflesberg, who is from Hennessy, Devil’s Bag and Halo lines. HIs dam is by Mizen Mast, who is from the Cozzene/Caro sire line. Those who follow TB lines that make good sport horses will truly appreciate this guy’s pedigree.

* we have some video of this guy popping over a tiny fence in his off season! Give it a look! We almost never get these!
