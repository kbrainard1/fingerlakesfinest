SUGARPAPI, 2021, 16.3h+, bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

Sugarpapi is the horse everyone waits for- big, bay with chrome, in your pocket personality, and unraced! This big guy was part of a large starvation case. When his current connections took him on, he was given an entire year to put on weight, grow, and train. And while Papi does have published works making him RRP eligible, he is not showing enough promise to actually race. Sugarpapi is his trainer’s “baby”, and she stated that he is an absolute sweetheart and a joy to have in the barn. He is reported to have no vices.

Sugarpapi showed off his loving nature as we tried to pose him for his photos, but he would much rather snuggle with our volunteer and his trainer. This horse is so much more impressive in person than we could show off in his photos. His connections report that this is a guy that would benefit from a consistent program. If he’s given rime off, he can be a bit playful, but even as evidenced in our jog video, given appropriate boundaries, he will come right back down. He also demonstrated a natural over track at the walk and a lofty, floaty trot. He was not fazed by the large puddles either.

Sugarpapi is by Maximus Mischief, a son of Into Mischief known for his athletic offspring with strong jumping ability. He is out of a Candy Ride mare whose offspring are sought after for their excellent brains and movement. This guy could succeed in any arena and his trainer will be really particular about who he goes to. He will come with a strict no auction and first right of refusal contract.

Price: $3,000 negotiable to the best of homes
