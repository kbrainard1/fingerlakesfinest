SOLO FLIGHT, 2021, 16.1h chestnut gelding

Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

Hubba hubba came out of a few of the volunteers mouths when this stunning 5 year old walked out. Solo Flight has presence and looks huge despite his estimated height of 16.1h.

Solo has raced 26 times without bringing home a win. His trainer says he doesn't need a pony horse because he is so chill and just "isn't a racehorse". He has clean legs and solid feet, but he does prefer to have front shoes on.

Reported to have no vices, he is good in his stall. Solo is an easy handler as he stood for photos very happily on a loose lead. He likes peppermints and is very respectful about taking them.

Solo Flight is by a sire that is becoming quite popular, Solomini. His offspring are proving to have excellent brains and to be great movers and jumpers! He’s out of a Sky Mesa mare, who has Storm Cat and Pulpit lines, which often through athletic babies with loads of personality. This flashy gelding is sure to get you noticed in any arena!
