BUSTINO SANTINO, 2020, 16h dark bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

Do you like bling? Dapples on a bay? How about a beautifully balanced hunter-type gelding? Bustino Sanino might be the horse you are looking for! This young man had our volunteers in stitches at his stall as he insisted on smooches and scritches and displayed his affection openly. He is reported to have no vices, and as we can attest, he is an absolute joy to be around. While he is “good to work with in his stall” he has not been turned out in a group yet.

Bustino Santino showed himself to be calm, well-mannered, and very respectful of his handler. He wanted to be part of the conversation and listened well when asked to stand and take his trot videos. He is said to be sound and to have no history of old injuries. He is reported to be a straightforward ride. While he gets a bit quick and choppy when he’s excited for his races, he demonstrated a more sweeping, flat-kneed jog for our video.

Bustino Santino is by the popular NY Sire, Bustin Stones, known for throwing loads of personality in his typically thick offspring. He is out of an It’s No Joke mare, who hails from Distorted Humor, who is known for his excellent brain and trainability. This guy definitely turned our volunteers' heads, and we are sure he will turn many others in just about any arena!
