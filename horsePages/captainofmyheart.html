CAPTAINOFMYHEART, 2020, 16.1+ bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

Do you want to get noticed in the hunter ring? Well, here you go! This sweet six-year-old gelding is about as classy as they come with his light bay coat, three white socks and a huge blaze across his face. His trainer describes him as a “real lover boy” who is good in his stall and in turnout with other horses and is said to “ to never have had an issue” of any sort. He is also said to be “clean as a whistle” and “never has he taken a bad step.” She went so far as to claim “this horse has never even had a dose of bute” in the time she has had him.

He came out quietly and stood to be admired and photographed and then jogged easily on a loose lead. He is nicely balanced with a good shoulder and a perfect height. He enjoyed being admired by our volunteers and was very interested in the mints used to help him pose. His jog was flat-kneed and toe-pointing. His floppy ears stayed forward and his tail wagged, showing what a happy, easy-going guy he is. He has been well-loves and it certainly shows!

Captainofmyheart is by Honorable Dillon, who is by Tapit, known for having offspring with loads of personality. He is out of a Freud mare who is known for ammy friendly kiddos with loads of talent in jumping. This loveable sweet guy is sure to excel in just about any discipline and is sure to get you noticed!

Please be prepared with references as they will be checked!

Preference will be given to those local to New York State and that plan to keep for at least a year.
