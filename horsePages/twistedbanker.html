TWISTED BANKER, 2023, 15.3h, chestnut gelding

Located at a farm near Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

If sweet, easy, and laid back all wrapped up in a fancy package sounds like what you are looking for, then Twisted Banker is the horse for you! This quiet boy had been gelded a week prior to our volunteer meeting him but he never knew he was a “boy”. He was only gelded as his connections knew that would make him easier to place. And as they always say: a good colt makes a GREAT gelding.

Twist hung out unbothered by literally anything going on around him during his photo shoot. He did come to his connections needing just a bit of weight and gelding him didn’t help, but just imagine how this horse will shine with a bit of groceries and some down time! For his jog video he happily jogged lazily next to his handler. Under saddle he is much the same, warming up without a pony and just jogs along like a show horse. We added a short clip from his last race so you can really see his lofty, fancy movement. He is reported to be injury and vice free. He did have tie back surgery, and he has raced afterwards and does not make a noise, so he should not have any disciplinary limitations.

Twisted Banker is by favorite New York sire, Central Banker and is out of a Discreet Cat mare with Empire Maker also on the bottom. This pedigree suggests exactly what he is: a kind, trainable guy with loads of potential and great movement! This horse is ready to be an amateur dream horse in any discipline of choice!

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse. Please see our post for more information.
