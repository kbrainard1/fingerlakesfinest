I’M MISCHIEVOUS, 2023, 15.3 h dark bay gelding

PLACED

Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

Mischievous? How about ‘I’m as cute as a button’? As he walked out from the shedrow, we all exclaimed, “how adorable!” and “too cute!”. We found this sweet boy to be a snuggler who was more interested in scritches from volunteers than our peppermints. He remained friendly and agreeable for his photoshoot. He was described to us as being, “sound, really easy, and a good boy to ride” with no stable vices. We observed a happy, friendly young gelding.

We loved his gorgeous, full forelock! He has a more refined build, and will be narrower than some to sit on, but is also deep through his body- which will still take up a lot of leg. And, at only three, he will continue to grow and fill out a bit more. Small and compact enough to be considered for some speed/ agility activities (such as polo or barrels), he’s certainly athletic, balanced, and a cute enough mover that we could see him going in any direction!

He is lightly raced, with 11 starts. After winning his first race he hasn't been running well at the next level of racing conditions, so his connections know it is time to let him find a new career. He displayed a loose, relaxed walk with a good overstep and had cute springy movement at the jog (with a happy, mischievous moment thrown in there- not surprising for a fit, good-feeling, three-year-old). But he remained polite, respectful, and was easily handled throughout; he just seemed to feel happy to show off a little for an audience.

He is sired by Honest Mischief, who is by Into Mischief out of a Seattle Slew mare. His dam is by City Zip.

If you are looking for a fun, happily mischievous young gelding, this cutie could be your boy!
