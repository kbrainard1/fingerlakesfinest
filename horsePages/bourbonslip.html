BOURBON SLIP, 2022, 15.3h bay filly

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

If pretty, young, and athletic are on your checklist, then Bourbon Slip may just be the girl for you! This girl is an absolute doll in her stall, enjoying scratches and peppermints from our volunteers. She is reported to have no vices and to be very easy to have in the barn. She has clearly shown her connections, however, that she is ready for life away from the track. She got a bit anxious coming out of her stall, sighting in on the track, but her connections state that she is completely relaxed at home at the farm.

While Bourbon was a bit nervous, she remained posable and respectful during her photo shoot. Our volunteers admired her compact build and chromed out coat. Despite her feelings about track life, she is still a straight-forward ride, with no bucks or rears. She is reported to be sound, with no previous injuries.

Bourbon Slip is by Freud, who is known for his ammy friendly, athletic offspring. She is out of a Run Away and Hide mare, who hails lines from City Zip, known for his kiddos with excellent brains and trainability. This sweet girl is ready to be someone’s best friend off the track and is ready to take you into any other discipline!
