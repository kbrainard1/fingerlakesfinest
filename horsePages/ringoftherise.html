RING OF THE RISE, 2022, 15.3+ h., dk bay f.

Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

This very pretty and refined War Dancer filly was definitely a friendly, in-your-pocket type and an easy girl to photograph. We’re told that she has no vices and is very easy to work with and around. And that’s what we observed- a friendly, relaxed, and sensible girl who was agreeable with everything that was asked. She was described as sound and injury-free and enjoys/ is great in turn-out. The exercise riders report that she is a “good girl”. She was even described as “not stupid” - she exhibits the maturity, good sense, and doesn’t get overly emotional. Many of us could appreciate a horse who can make some smart choices on their own!

This feminine, well-balanced, and leggy girl has an elegant neck. Only four, she still has growing and filling out yet to do- so we think she will blossom into an even lovelier mare. In her video, she displayed a relaxed swing in her back and flowy movement during a very rhythmic jog. We were told that on the track she has the early speed and quickness that she perhaps could do barrels or polo. But we think that she’s also extremely pretty, with the light, airy, fluid movement and the steady temperament to excel as a hunter or dressage princess. She’s a gorgeous filly that is athletic and sensible enough to do just about anything- and look darn pretty doing it!

In 14 starts, she has a single win to her credit. Her trainer suggests that perhaps she would be more competitive running on turf, but as the season is starting to wind down, her owner has decided that maybe it is time to move her along instead. Ring of the Rise is by War Dancer, out of a Precise End mare with Blushing Groom also making an appearance in her pedigree.

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse. Please see our post pinned to the top of our FB page for more information.
