DOHA DESERT, 2023, 16.1+h, bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

If easy, kind, and sweet is what you’re looking for, then look no farther than Doha Desert! This handsome boy is only three years old and has made it clear that racing is not his jam. He calmly walked out and visited with us, charming all of our volunteers, and even a shopper who deemed his personality as “sleepy”. While that may not have made him a great racehorse, it certainly makes him a pleasure to have around the barn! He is reported to have no vices.

Doha stood for his pictures and trotted for his video as if he had done it one thousand times before. This gelding is another one who simply follows along on a loose leadline. His trainer says he is calm to work around and very easy to ride. He is reported to be sound with no history of injuries or lameness and ready to start a new career while finishing growing up.

Doha Desert is by Midshipman, who’s offspring have been noted for their size and good brains. He is out of a Street Sense mare, who’s also known for their trainability. Here is your opportunity for a young, lightly raced horse with loads of potential for any career outside of racing!

Offered at $2,000.
