SJ'S FAITH, 2022, 16.2 h., bay m.

Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

This gorgeous girls photos speak for themselves! Tall, thick and stunning in just about every way. SJ's Faith is an unraced four year old who has been owned by the same person since she was a yearling. She is reported to have no vices and is very easy to have in the barn! And boy does she love her meals (and treats)! She will remain in race training unless someone lucky snatches her up before then!

Her trainer reports that SJ is a snuggler and respectful. She walked and jogged on a loose lead to prove that. SJ is currently barefoot on the hinds. She is said to have good feet, but she is funny about having her hinds worked on. SJ's has both of her leads and her trainer was tempted to throw western tack on her to be a pony horse! She is reported to have no past injuries.

SJ's Faith is by Well Spelled who hails Tail of the Cat and Quiet American lines. She is out of a Bluegrass Cat mare. This girl is the whole package- gorgeous, well minded and a blank slate ready to become your next show partner! Don't let this one get away!
