STRAND ROAD, 2022, 16.3h bay gelding

PLACED

If you are looking for long and tall, Strand Road might be your guy. This 16.3+ guy has a gorgeous shoulder, a lovely overtrack at the walk and it is clear he knows how to use his hind end when jogging. He has solid feet, solid bone and a serious dose of bling to boot. His trainer said he has “has zero issues.” All that and he probably has an inch or two more to grow before he finishes out. We imagine him in the hunt field or the show ring! He is quiet in his stall and was happy to just hang with us on a loose line, nuzzle for mints and be admired. He is reported to have no vices, goes out to the races with no pony, and a very good boy in every way.

Strand Road is by Astern, who is by the highly desirable Medaglia D’oro, sought after for athleticism, jumping ability, movement, and incredible brains. Eventers have loved Astern offspring. He’s out of a Thunder Gulch mare, also known for trainability and jumping ability. Despite his “speed-bred” pedigree, he has shown no interest in using it on the track, so let’s get this gorgeous guy his new human!
