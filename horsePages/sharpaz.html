SHARPAZ, 2020, 17h dark bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

Sharpaz checks every single box. Tall? Check. Dark? Check. Incredibly handsome? Check. This is the type of guy that will absolutely get you noticed in the show arena as he simply demands presence. He is reported to be easy to work with and around, with no vices, save some grumpy faces at the track. He is far happier at the farm!

Sharpaz enjoyed our volunteers’ attention as he posed perfectly for his photo shoot. Even though they don’t convey on the cloudy day, his coat shone and was full of dapples. He is reported to have no previous injuries, and to be a straightforward ride with a workman-like attitude. He showed off his natural over-track at the walk and his lofty, floaty trot for his video.

Sharpaz is by Sharp Azteca, who is by the sought-after Freud, known for their jumping ability and big personalities. He is out of a Maclean’s Music mare, known for their excellent brains and movement. This guy is a showstopper and sure to get you noticed in any arena!
