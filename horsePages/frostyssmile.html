FROSTY'S SMILE, 2023, 16h dark bay filly

Did you get a good look at these pictures? When this stunning filly came out for her session we all took a collective deep breath! What a beauty! She immediately reminded us all of Bustino Santino who we listed a few weeks ago and had a new home within hours of his listing. You can be sure she, too, will not be available for long. This stunner needs to be headed to the hunter ring - the children’s’ hunter ring -what with her fantastic good kooks, size, her balanced confirmation, solid feet and a serious dose of bling. She certainly enjoyed all the attention - and treats- we gave her and, in turn seemed happy to hang out on a loose line for pix and a quiet jog video. She is said to be “good in her stall, energetic, sweet and a happy horse.” Despite being described as “just slow” by her trainer, she then went on to suggest she’d be “a great barrel horse” given her size. That said, she still has some growing and maturing to look forward to as she heads to the hunter or dressage ring.

Those in the know were also impressed by her pedigree despite the fact that she does not seem to have inherited her daddy’s (Frosted) reputation for speed, but her Tapit lines come through in her movement and stunning looks.
