FROSTY KARINA, 2020, 16.2h chestnut mare

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

This mare is so very nice! Her trainer described her as “a perfect mare who does nothing wrong except “she gets nervous at the track and does not want to race.” She’s a true liver chestnut, very sweet, and a beautiful hunter type mover by Frost Giant, out of a Smarty Jones mare — a great sport horse pedigree for sure! We listed her half brother out of the same mare, named She Speaks Well, who went on to do very well at last year's RRP in dressage. Before that we listed the half sister, Georgiana's Best, also a dark liver chestnut, and also a sweet cuddlebug who is now packing kids around the hunter courses at local shows in Pennsylvania.

Frosty Karina did not start training or race until her 5th year. Her breeder often starts her horses very late — sometimes doesn't even introduce them to saddle and bridle until they are 3 or even 4. So her late start is actually a huge “plus” for maturity and longevity in her next career. She has a strong overtrack at the walk, a powerful push from behind at the jog, solid feet, a beautiful shoulder and her legs are said to be “pristinely clean.” She has been around kids, is good in her stall and her trainer says “he will miss this one when she is gone.”
