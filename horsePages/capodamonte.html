CAPO DA MONTE, 2023, 15.3 h., ch m.

Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

If a sweet chestnut filly is your type, look no further. Capo Da Monte is lightly raced with only 3 starts. She reportedly came to her current trainer underweight but has since put on over 100lbs in the past 2 months. She is reported to have no vices and to be an absolute love in her stall.

Capo is reported to simply not have the capacity for racing. A couple of her races had her in the second-place slot, until she just lost interest and faded to the back. She has a lot of energy and can get stressed in certain situations. This presents itself as jigging and sweating. The track life might simply be too much for this sweetheart. But even still she is reported to be good to ride and to have no previous injuries.

Capo was very well behaved for us volunteers. She greatly enjoyed the attention and the scritches given during her photo shoot. She presented a nice easy trot on a loose lead.

Capo is by Capo Kane who boasts lines from Street Sense and Hard Spun, thus likely inheriting their excellent brains and athleticism. She is out of a He’s Had Enough mare with Tapit and Dixieland Band lines, ensuring she will definitely have a ton of personality! This sweet girl is ready for life off the racetrack- don’t let her get away!

Price: $1,500 neg
