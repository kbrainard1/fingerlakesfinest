LAST VINTAGE, 2022, 16.1h, bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

Take a look at this big, bay, beautiful gelding just asking to get off the track and do something more fun and interesting. The only issue the trainer has with this guy is that he doesn’t seem interested in finishing his races. “He starts well out of the gate, runs with the pack and then just fades to the back and doesn’t seem interested in finishing the race.” Last Vintage is reported to have no vices and easy to have in the barn. As with most horses from his connections, he’s been around the kids and absolutely adores them!

We volunteers were all quite interested in him. He came out on a loose line and stood to drink in all the attention and the mints. He has four solid feet and a nice, relaxed trot, again, on a loose lead. He is reported to be just as straightforward a ride, with no bucking or rearing.

Last Vintage is by Anthony’s Cross who is by the acclaimed Indian Charlie, known for throwing offspring that are very amateur friendly, trainable, and have success in just about every arena. He is out of a Bertrando mare with Relaunch and Halo lines. Last Vintage is a young, lightly raced guy that will likely age just as well as a fine wine!

Price: $3,500 neg
