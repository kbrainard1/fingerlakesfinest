TROUBLER, 2022, 16.1h bay filly

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack Finger Finger Lakes Racetrack Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

Troubler is anything but that based on our recent visit. Rather, she is a “well-loved lap dog” of a filly who “is good with her handlers, good with her neighbors and is “easy in the stall and easy to get on.” Her trainer says “even after days of no work she behaves and is no trouble at all.” She is nicely balanced, compact, has solid feet, “frames up nicely” and is said to be sound with no issues. She visited with us on a loose line, enjoyed the attention and jogged easily, proving herself to be a sweetheart of a filly. She “has not been running well “and the owner “sees no reason to winter her over for another season” so she is primed for the RRP Makeover or whatever new career awaits her.

Bubbles, as she is affectionately known, has strong sport horse influence on both sides of her pedigree: Into Mischief offers “incredible athleticism, quick reflexes, powerful hindquarters, and trainable minds” while on her damside she is by a Mr. Greely mare who also carries an impressive sporthorse reputation.
