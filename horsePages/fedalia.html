FEDALIA, 2018, 16.1h grey mare

Located at Finger Lakes Race Track, Farmington NY (near Rochester)

She won our volunteers' hearts with her beautiful appearance and her sweet demeanor. She just wanted to cuddle with us and crawl in our pockets looking for treats. Her trainer told us that she is a very relaxed and friendly and patient mare, very good to handle and work around. We got to see that for ourselves, as one of our volunteers handled her for her photos and jog video, and Fedalia was indeed patient with us as we tried to set her up in pretty poses. And she was so polite to let a stranger jog her, attentive to every request. She showed an even smooth trot. Her trainer told us that Fedalia has some mild arthritis in her left knee, and while she has been racing on it for a long time, he realizes it is time to let her move on to a less strenuous career. While she may need some maintenance, we think she will be well worth it considering how pretty and sweet she is. She was quite successful earlier in her racing career, accumulating 6 wins and multiple placings, with over $166,000 in earnings. Her sir is Feb Biz, a son of Giant's Causeway — both excellent sport horse sires. Her dam is from Unbridled Song lines, and has produced stakes winning racehorses.

The trainer has asked Finger Lakes Finest to be the first point of contact to check references, so if you are interested in the gorgeous sweetheart Fedalia, send us a PM and we will chat with you about her.

Contact: PM Finger Lakes Finest Thoroughbreds on Facebook

Price: $2500 negotiable

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse. Please see our post pinned to the top of our FB page for more information.
