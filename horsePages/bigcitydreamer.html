BIG CITY DREAMER, 2022, 16h, bay filly

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

You know you’ve got a special horse when not only is she a trainer favorite, but she is a favorite of the trainer’s children, and Big City Dreamer is just that. Dreamer is so sweet and kind in her stall and at the farm that their son is frequently found with her. She is reported to have no vices, to be easy to have in the barn. She turns out well with others.

Dreamer is one of those “doesn’t look like a thoroughbred” thoroughbreds. Her connections affectionately call her a “tank”. On the track, all that can be said for her is that she is SLOW, so slow, in fact, that she unfortunately doesn’t even have a published work. She is reported to be a straightforward ride and to have no prior injuries. For her jog video she showed off her casual and laid-back nature as she took some encouraging to move forward. Once she did, though, she showed off her steady, flat-kneed jog.

Dreamer is by Big Brown, a favorite for offspring with jumping ability and their amateur friendly brains. She is out of a Relato Del Gato mare, who has Tale of the Cat lines, known for their athletic ability. Big City Dreamer is an amateur dream horse that is ready to become someone’s heart horse

Price: $3,500 negotiable
