JUST A GUY, 2022, 16.2h, dark bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (Near Rochester)

Here we have a four-year old black beauty who has been with his trainer for almost his entire life. She is clearly in love with him and could not stop saying what a lover he is and how much she adores him. Over and over she emphasized “what you see is what you get,” he is “a lover,” ”a doll” and “a sweetheart.” What she couldn’t say was that he is a racehorse; “ “He is just not fast enough and seems to have no inclination to race.” He is reported to have no vices and clearly a pleasure to have in the barn.

He is reported to have no history of injuries and his connections would like to keep him that way so they are moving him on in search of his next career, “even if he wins next week.” He impressed us with his sweet personality, his kind eye and his lovely trot and overstride, which he was able to show off despite feeling his oats early in the cool morning. He is reported to be a straightforward ride with no silly business.

Just a Guy is by Catholic Boy who is regally bred with More Than Ready and Bernardini lines, both known for their amateur friendly brains and stunning movement. He is out of a Speightstown mare who is also known for their trainability or athleticism. Just a Guy should be the right guy for just about anyone!

Offered at $3,500
