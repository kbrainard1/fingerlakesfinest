CRUISING CAT, 2023, 16.1 h bay gelding

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

If you’re looking for the horse that everyone in the barn wants to take home, then look no farther than Cruising Cat! Every single person in the barn that our volunteers came across couldn’t stop gushing about how wonderful this sweet guy is- most of them going so far as to say that if they had their own place, they would keep him for themselves. He is reported to have no vices, to be super easy to be around, and very clean in his stall. He’s also an eater!

For his photo shoot, on a cool, breezy day when many other horses around him were feeling their oats, Cat didn’t want to stand still- but only because he wanted to crawl right into his handler’s pocket. When we could get him standing off he showed himself off well. He is reported to be a straightforward ride. After his last race, Cat sustained a hairline fracture in his left knee. X-rays are available to serious parties. He will likely need 4-6 WEEKS stall rest. For this reason, we only obtained a walk video.

Cruising Cat is by Catalina Cruiser, who hails lines from Union Rags and Mineshaft, both known for to excel in jumping and for their trainability. He is out of a Tiznow mare, known for their athleticism and movement. Give this guy the time he needs and he should be able to excel in just about any discipline and he will absolutely be your very best friend.
