QUEEN ISLA, 2023, 15.3h+, bay filly

Located at Finger Lakes Racetrack, Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

If quiet, sound, and sweet-as-can-be are boxes on your check list, then you won’t want to miss Queen Isla! Isla is a kind filly that only had one start as a. 2 year old and is showing no promise this year as a 3 year old so her connections feel it is best to let her go find another job. Isla is easy in her stall, nice to be around, and is reported to have no vices.

Even on a dreary, breezy day after only walking for the week, she jogged politely beside her handler. She showed off a natural over track at the walk and a big, lofty trot. She is reported to be good to ride on the track, with no bad habits. She is reported to be sound with clean, tight legs, and no prior injuries.

Queen Isla is by King for a Day who is by Uncle Mo, who’s babies are sought after for their brain and movement. She is out of a Street Cry mare. Queen Isla is nearly a blank slate just waiting for her new career. Don’t let this one get away!
